03

The page says “4.8 stars. 2,000+ Google reviews” and “Calls Answered 24/7”. 04

The homepage is slow in the load test.

In a simulated slow-connection load test, your homepage's first content appeared at 4.4 seconds and its largest part, the hero background image, at 6.3 seconds. The page is light, about 0.8 MB, and the browser was tied up for only 20 milliseconds. One run.

05

Several page titles repeat your name.

The homepage title is “Precision Overhead Door of Las Vegas - America's #1 Garage Door Service - Precision Overhead Door of Las Vegas”, 115 characters. The Locations, About and Contact titles also repeat the name. The Reviews page description is “Reviews Archive - Precision Overhead Door of Las Vegas”.

06
